VSM builder and game

Record your own process, add improvement ideas, then play to find the bottleneck and test which ideas raise flow efficiency the most.

How it works

  1. Enter your current-state steps below: owner, hands-on process time, wait time before the step, and %C&A.
  2. Add improvement ideas. Each idea sets a new process time and wait time for one step.
  3. Press Start game. Pick the step you think is the biggest bottleneck, then choose which ideas to apply.
  4. Compare your choice with the best possible one, then view the full value stream map for the current or future state.
  5. Save your work as a file to reload later.

Your work also autosaves in this browser. Times are hours; a working day is counted as 8 hours.
